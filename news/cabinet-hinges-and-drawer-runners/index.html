When one cupboard door or drawer stops working

A single failed fitting does not always mean a whole kitchen or wardrobe needs replacing.

By Ellis Services Group · Canberra carpentry advice
In brief

A single failed fitting does not always mean a whole kitchen or wardrobe needs replacing. The component and any moisture or support issue should be checked before the repair scope is agreed.

Identify the failed part

Check the hinge, runner, fixing points and surrounding panel. A replacement fitting must suit the existing cabinet.

Measure before sourcing

Photos and approximate dimensions help determine whether compatible hardware is available.

Look for water damage

If a kickboard or panel has swollen after a leak, the source should be dealt with before fitting a replacement.

What to send with your enquiry

Include a photo of the whole item, a close-up of the affected area, your Canberra suburb and any access details. If you are unsure whether the issue is carpentry work, describe what you see and we can discuss the next step.
