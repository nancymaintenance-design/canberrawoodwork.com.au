Why rotten timber can return after a repair

A local timber patch is only part of the answer if the source of moisture remains.

By Ellis Services Group · Canberra carpentry advice
In brief

A local timber patch is only part of the answer if the source of moisture remains. The component and any moisture or support issue should be checked before the repair scope is agreed.

Find the water path

Look for repeated wetting, failed coatings, damaged joints or leaks around the affected component.

Check beyond the surface

Paint may hide soft timber. The repair boundary should reach sound material and preserve the function of the component.

Separate related trades

A gutter, roof or plumbing issue may need the appropriate trade before timber is repaired and finished.

What to send with your enquiry

Include a photo of the whole item, a close-up of the affected area, your Canberra suburb and any access details. If you are unsure whether the issue is carpentry work, describe what you see and we can discuss the next step.
