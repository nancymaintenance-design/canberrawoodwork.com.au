Repair an existing deck or plan a new one?

Board-only work and a new deck are different scopes with different checks.

By Ellis Services Group · Canberra carpentry advice
In brief

Board-only work and a new deck are different scopes with different checks. The component and any moisture or support issue should be checked before the repair scope is agreed.

Assess the existing frame

Boards, joists, bearers, posts and connections should be considered separately.

Compare outcomes

A limited repair may be sensible where the support is sound; extensive defects may call for a new design and quote.

Check before building

Dimensions, boundaries, approvals and any ACT practitioner requirements should be confirmed before a new deck or extension starts.

What to send with your enquiry

Include a photo of the whole item, a close-up of the affected area, your Canberra suburb and any access details. If you are unsure whether the issue is carpentry work, describe what you see and we can discuss the next step.
