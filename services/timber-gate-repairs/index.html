What the work can include

A timber gate repair concerns the gate leaf, its frame, support post, hinges, latch and clearances as one moving assembly. A gate can drag because its frame has sagged, a post has shifted or a fixing has pulled out. The appropriate work may be adjustment, replacement of a timber component or a new gate discussion if the existing frame is beyond repair. Describe whether it is a small garden gate, a side-access gate or a larger opening.

What homeowners commonly notice

Record where the gate rubs, whether the gap changes from top to bottom, whether the latch reaches its keeper and whether the post moves when the gate is gently operated. Show any split timber around hinges and any softness at the post base. Mention if the problem began after paving, a fence repair or wet weather. These details help narrow the first questions but do not prove which part must be replaced.

How the scope is assessed

The gate needs to be viewed both open and closed so support, alignment and hardware can be considered together. Hinge and latch alignment should be checked against the gate frame and support post, rather than corrected at one point while the opening remains out of square. Refitting a hinge to damaged timber without sound fixing material may be short-lived. If the post has moved, adjusting the latch alone will not settle the cause. Material thickness, gate weight, swing direction, ground clearance and nearby fence rails all matter. A quote should state whether hardware, post work, timber repair and finishing are included.

Where this repair may stop

Timber fitting on a manual gate is separate from a motorised drive or wiring. Access control and automatic safety devices require relevant specialists as well. If a powered gate also has damaged timber, identify the timber issue without assuming its electrical or mechanical system can be adjusted as part of the same repair. A gate attached to a shared fence may also involve owner agreement about boundary work. If the support is masonry or a structural element, fixing details need separate assessment. No repair should be presented as a security guarantee based only on a photograph. The gate must operate safely and clear its path before a final scope is agreed.

Canberra conditions to mention

Timber gates can swell after rain and shrink during Canberra’s dry periods, while sun exposure changes coatings and joints. A side gate squeezed between a house and fence may have little tolerance for that movement. Soil movement or persistent moisture at a post base can change alignment independently of the gate leaf. Tell us whether the fault is seasonal and whether the opening is exposed to direct rain or irrigation.

Prepare your enquiry

Send a wide view of the closed gate from both sides where possible, one open view of the hinges and post, and close images of the latch or damaged joint. Approximate width and height, swing direction, ground clearance, suburb and access route are useful. State whether the gate is manual or motorised and whether the adjoining fence moves. You can attach photos to the enquiry; further material can be shared later if requested.
