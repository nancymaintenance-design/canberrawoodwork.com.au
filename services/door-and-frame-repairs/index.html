Questions about this service

Can a timber door be adjusted after new flooring?

Often the new floor changes the available clearance. The door construction and required gap should be checked before trimming.

Should I call a carpenter or locksmith if a door will not latch?

If the timber door or frame has shifted, a carpenter may be appropriate. A faulty lock mechanism should be assessed by a locksmith.

What the work can include

Timber door and frame repairs cover the door leaf and the parts that let it swing, close and latch: jambs, stops, hinges, strike area and threshold. A repair may be a local adjustment, a sound fixing point, a replacement timber section or a discussion about a door that is no longer practical to retain. Internal and external doors have different exposure and security considerations. Describe the whole opening, not only the corner that rubs, so the possible work is not narrowed too early.

What homeowners commonly notice

Useful clues include scraping at one edge, a changing gap above the door, a latch that misses the plate, a hinge that moves when the door is lifted, or soft timber at the foot of a jamb. Record whether the door catches only after rain, after new flooring, or throughout the year. If water enters at an external opening, photograph the threshold and adjacent wall as well. These signs help frame the enquiry but cannot establish the cause from a photo alone.

How the scope is assessed

Assessment starts with the door closed: compare hinge-side and latch-side gaps, floor-to-door clearance, hinge positions and how securely the jamb is fixed. If new flooring has narrowed the gap, measure at the tightest point before deciding whether trimming is appropriate. A loose hinge or shifted jamb may explain a scrape without removing timber. Split fixing timber may need a local repair and new fixing strategy. At an external door, check the weather seal, threshold and water path. The proposed scope should say whether hardware, surface preparation and repainting are included.

Where this repair may stop

A failed lock cylinder or electronic access device belongs with a locksmith or relevant specialist. Broken glass, metal or fire-rated door assemblies and compliance-controlled openings may need another trade or a different process. If movement appears to come from the surrounding structure, a door adjustment alone is unlikely to be an adequate answer. No repair or adjustment should be represented as a security or weatherproofing guarantee before the full opening has been assessed.

Canberra conditions to mention

Timber can respond to Canberra’s dry periods and wet weather differently across seasons. A door that binds only after rain may point to exposure at the threshold or frame as well as ordinary movement. Older homes may have non-standard jamb and architrave profiles, while a recent floor finish can reduce clearance. Tell us when the behaviour changed and whether the opening faces weather; these facts are more useful than guessing which part is at fault.

Prepare your enquiry

Provide a photograph of the door closed, one of the full frame with the door open, and close views of the rubbing edge, hinge or damaged timber. State whether it is an entry or internal door, its approximate width, the location of any new flooring and what the latch does. Include your Canberra suburb and access details. You can attach photos to the enquiry; further material can be shared later if requested.
