Skirting boards finish the base of a wall. Architraves trim around doors and windows. Both should be matched to the adjoining profile where possible.

What the work can include

Skirting boards finish the meeting of wall and floor; architraves finish the edges of door and window openings. Repair work may be a short damaged section, reinstallation after flooring, a new run in a renovated room or a profile match beside older timber. Corners, returns and the junction with existing paint or floor coverings can take more thought than the length suggests. State whether the aim is a like-for-like repair or a changed look across the room.

What homeowners commonly notice

Show missing sections, open mitres, swelling at the floor line, cracked paint at joints and trim that has pulled away from the wall. Note if the problem followed new flooring, plaster repairs or water damage. A wide photograph of the wall and opening makes the profile and finish easier to understand than a close-up alone. Visible staining may indicate moisture behind the trim, so avoid assuming the board is the only item involved.

How the scope is assessed

A suitable scope considers profile, height and finish alongside board thickness, material, lengths, corners and the wall or substrate behind the fixing. Existing profiles may be difficult to match precisely; a sample or clear end-on photo helps discuss options. The flooring edge and wall finish determine how a replacement meets what stays in place. Removal can reveal uneven plaster, flooring gaps or damaged framing that changes the fitting sequence. The quote should distinguish supply, fitting, filling, caulking and painting so the expected finish is clear.

Where this repair may stop

Active leaks, damaged flooring and wall repairs beyond the timber trim need their own scope. Painting may be included only if stated, and a close profile match should not be promised before a suitable product is identified. If an architrave sits beside a sticking or damaged door frame, the opening may need repair first. Wet-area junctions can have waterproofing considerations that should be checked by an appropriate practitioner.

Canberra conditions to mention

Established Canberra homes often mix original and later trim profiles, sometimes within the same room. A short replacement can stand out when its height or finish differs from adjacent boards. Seasonal movement may open fine joints without meaning every piece needs replacing, while water from a bathroom or entry can cause real swelling. Tell us the age or renovation history if known, and whether matching nearby rooms is important to you.

Prepare your enquiry

Send a wide photograph of each wall or opening, a close image of the profile end or damaged joint, and approximate lengths and board height. Note whether flooring is already installed, whether you have spare matching trim and what finish you expect. Include the Canberra suburb, room count and access details. You can attach photos to the enquiry; further material can be shared later if requested.
